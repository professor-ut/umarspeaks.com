About

Call me a Sports Pracademic.

I have spent more than 15 years on both sides of sport: building programs on the ground and studying the systems behind them. I see sport as one of the most powerful tools we have to form people and grow communities. I also see what sports takes from athletes when no one is watching.

That is why my work sits at the intersection of NIL, athlete entrepreneurship, HBCU sport and small-market sports activation. The goal is simple: help athletes, families and organizations compete for the good in life, not just the next contract.

Educator

Assistant Professor of Instruction in Sport Administration at Ohio University. I prepare the next generation of sport leaders in the classroom, through one-on-one career coaching and through international study programs, including study abroad in Australia.

Researcher

Ed.D. in Sports Leadership. My doctoral research produced the NIL Activity Scale, the foundation of the NIL Readiness Score. I also developed the Six-P Pipeline framework.

Builder

At Saint Augustine's University I built programs where none existed:

  • The first HBCU cycling team in the country (2020), named USA Cycling's Club of the Year and covered by CNN, PBS and Andscape.
  • The first HBCU women's rowing team in the country (2022).
  • The first collegiate virtual sports program in the country (2021).

Today I advise organizations through Umar T. Muhammad, LLC.
