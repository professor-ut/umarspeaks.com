Two frameworks, one athlete

What the system does vs. what your child needs

The Six-P Pipeline names how revenue sport moves athletes from Project to Product. Long-Term Athlete Development (LTAD) describes how athletes should grow at each age. The check-up places your child on both.

ProjectLTAD: Active Start / FUNdamentalsWatch for: early specialization replacing play
PotentialLTAD: Learn to TrainWatch for: outside investment during key skill years
ProfileLTAD: Train to TrainWatch for: rankings, social media and NIL during puberty. Highest-risk overlap.
ProspectLTAD: Train to CompeteWatch for: recruiting that treats your child as an acquisition
ProfessionalLTAD: Train to WinWatch for: being managed as an asset, not developed as a person
ProductLTAD: Active for LifeWatch for: a career ending without a plan for life after

Ages are guides, not deadlines. Development timing varies by sport and by child.
