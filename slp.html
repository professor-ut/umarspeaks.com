Sport as Lived Practice

Sport changes people. It shapes who you become, what you believe about yourself and what you owe the people around you. Yet no institution in sport has ever been held accountable for what it actually does to a human being. SLP exists to change that.

What we believe

Sports takes. It should give back.

Sport forms people.

What you experience on the field follows you into every room for the rest of your life.

Athletes deserve a return on what they gave.

Years of discipline and identity investment are worth more than a jersey and a highlight reel.

Communities deserve a return too.

Every dollar, every drive to practice and every volunteer Saturday is an investment.

Practitioners must meet a higher standard.

Power over young lives requires conscience, not just competence.
