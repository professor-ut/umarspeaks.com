Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Umar T. Muhammad, LLC ("we," "us") collects through umarspeaks.com and utmuhammad.com, how we use it and the choices you have. We keep it simple: we collect only what we need to send you what you asked for, and we never sell your information.

What We Collect

Information you give us:

  • Newsletter: your email address, and if you choose, your first name and role (athlete, parent, coach, student or sport industry professional).
  • Contact and speaking requests: your name, email, organization and the details you share about your question or event.
  • Assessments: when the NIL Readiness Score and Six-P Pipeline Check-Up open, your answers and the email address you use to receive results. The Six-P Check-Up never asks for your child's name, photo or contact details. It asks only for age, grade and sport.
  • Purchases and memberships: when the shop opens, payment is handled by a third-party payment processor. We do not see or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically: like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and our font provider (Google Fonts) receive basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the page. We do not currently use advertising cookies or visitor tracking.

How We Use It

  • To send the newsletter and the resources you signed up for.
  • To answer your questions and respond to speaking, workshop and advisory requests.
  • To deliver assessment results and improve our research and tools. Research use is in combined, anonymous form only.
  • To deliver purchases and manage memberships, once available.

Who We Share It With

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the site, and only for that purpose:

  • Kit, our email platform, which stores newsletter subscribers and sends our emails.
  • A form service that delivers contact and booking messages to our inbox.
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting) and Google Fonts (typefaces).
  • A payment processor, once the shop opens.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Email Choices

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You can leave at any time, and we will stop emailing you.

Young Athletes and Children

Our newsletter and assessments are meant for adults and teens age 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Athletes under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission to take the NIL Readiness Score. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, please reach us through the contact page and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Send your request through the contact page and we will respond within 30 days.

How Long We Keep It and How We Protect It

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires. We use reputable service providers and limit who can access your information, but no website or email system can guarantee complete security.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be announced in the newsletter.
